前置对话沉淀的论点与论据

论点 · 补贴

Subsidies lower the price of fresh produce, making a balanced diet affordable for everyone.

补贴降低新鲜农产品的价格，让所有人都吃得起均衡饮食。

论据 · 补贴

For low-income families, price is the biggest barrier to healthy eating.

对低收入家庭而言，价格是健康饮食的最大障碍。

论点 · 征税

A tax on sugary drinks and junk food raises their price and directly discourages over-consumption.

对含糖饮料和垃圾食品征税抬高价格，直接抑制过度消费。

论据 · 征税

After the UK introduced its sugar tax, manufacturers cut the sugar content of drinks significantly.

英国开征糖税后，饮料厂商显著降低了产品含糖量。

事例

Hungary's junk-food tax funds free school meals and public health programmes.

匈牙利的垃圾食品税资助了免费校餐和公共健康项目。

让步

Admittedly, food taxes can burden the poor, but they ultimately guide healthier choices.

诚然食品税会加重穷人负担，但最终会引导更健康的选择。
